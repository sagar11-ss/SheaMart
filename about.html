What is SheaMart?

SheaMart is an online retail management system designed to provide customers with a simple and convenient shopping experience.

Customers can browse products, place orders and track their order status. The system also helps administrators manage customer orders efficiently.

❓

What Problem Does It Solve?

Traditional shopping and manual order management can take time and make it difficult for customers to know the status of their orders.

SheaMart provides a simple digital solution where customers can easily access product information and track their orders.
